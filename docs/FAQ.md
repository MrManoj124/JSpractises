# Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

## General Questions

### Q: What is the Accuracy Analyzer?
**A**: It's a tool to test sorting algorithms with your custom numbers and find which algorithm works best for your specific data.

### Q: Is it free?
**A**: Yes! It's completely free and open-source.

### Q: Do I need to install anything?
**A**: No! Just open the HTML file in your browser.

### Q: Can I use it offline?
**A**: Charts require Chart.js from CDN, but the core functionality works offline.

---

## Technical Questions

### Q: What's the maximum number count?
**A**: 10,000 numbers maximum.

### Q: How fast is it?
**A**: Typically under 1 second for 10,000 numbers.

### Q: Can I sort negative numbers?
**A**: Yes! All algorithms handle negative numbers.

### Q: What about floating-point numbers?
**A**: Yes, decimals are supported (except Radix Sort optimized for integers).

### Q: Which algorithm should I use?
**A**: Use the tool to test! But generally:
- Quick Sort: Best overall
- Merge Sort: Guaranteed consistency
- Others: Educational purposes

---

## Usage Questions

### Q: How do I input numbers?
**A**: Three ways:
1. Manual: Type comma-separated numbers
2. Random: Generate with custom range
3. Presets: Use built-in datasets

### Q: Can I export results?
**A**: Yes! Click "Export Report (CSV)" on the results page.

### Q: How do I interpret the results?
**A**: Lower execution time = better
Look at all metrics, not just speed.

### Q: Can I test with my own dataset?
**A**: Yes! Paste your numbers (comma-separated) in the input field.

---

## Performance Questions

### Q: Why is analysis slow?
**A**: Usually due to large dataset size. Reduce to under 5000 numbers.

### Q: Why different results each run?
**A**: Small variations are normal. Run multiple times for average.

### Q: Which chart type is best?
**A**: Radar for overview, Bar/Line for details.

---

## Troubleshooting Questions

### Q: Charts won't show?
**A**: Refresh page and check internet connection for Chart.js.

### Q: Numbers keep disappearing?
**A**: Copy/paste fresh data and try again.

### Q: My browser crashes?
**A**: Use fewer numbers (max 5000 recommended for stability).

---

## Educational Questions

### Q: Is this good for learning?
**A**: Absolutely! Best way to understand sorting algorithms.

### Q: Can I use this in class?
**A**: Yes! Great teaching tool with visual comparisons.

### Q: How do I explain results to others?
